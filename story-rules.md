# Story Rules

1. **QUESTION FIRST** - Open on a moment that makes the viewer ask why; hold the answer back.
2. **MAKE US CARE** - Early on, the lead does something kind or fair and is treated badly for it.
3. **THE VIEWER KNOWS MORE** - Show the viewer one danger or secret the lead can't see.
4. **EVERY BEAT TURNS** - Each beat flips good to bad (or bad to good); link beats with "but" or "therefore", never "and then".
5. **PAY OFF, THEN REOPEN** - Give the hoped-for win, then open a bigger question and stop.
